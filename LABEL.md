# The Doorman

Danny Girdish and Alexander Bay Baltierrez | HC-SR04, Arduino Nano, red LED, breadboard and jumpers | 2026

It notices how close a body comes. It calls you Near, Far or Nobody. Near holds the red light steady; Far makes it blink slowly; Nobody leaves it dark. Danny owns Near, Alexander owns Far, and Nobody belongs to the group. It refuses to remember who came before you or to count visits. The word is not a fact about you; it is the name this small machine assigns from distance alone.
